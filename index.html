GTA 6 Central Poster

A private publishing tool for the owner of the GTA 6 Central channel. It is not a public app and is not offered to other users.

What it does

The owner signs in with their own TikTok account, then uploads finished GTA 6 gameplay clips to that same account. Each upload arrives in TikTok as a draft so the owner can review it and publish it themselves. Nothing is posted automatically.

How a typical upload works

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit). The tool reads basic profile information so it can show which account is connected.
  2. The owner selects an edited gameplay clip and starts an upload (Content Posting API, video.upload).
  3. The clip is sent to the owner’s TikTok account as a draft.
  4. The owner reviews the draft in TikTok and, if they choose, publishes it there.

Integrations

Sign-in and posting are handled through Composio, an integration platform that manages the TikTok OAuth connection on the owner’s behalf. The tool requests only the scopes it needs: user.info.basic and video.upload.

Who it is for

This app is used only by the operator of GTA 6 Central (YouTube @gta6central777, Instagram @jalgaming_) to publish their own clips to their own TikTok account.
