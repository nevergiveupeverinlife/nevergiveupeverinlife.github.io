Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026. This policy explains what data GTA 6 Central Poster uses, why it is used, and how you can delete it or revoke access.

1. Who this applies to

GTA 6 Central Poster is a private tool used by the owner of the GTA 6 Central channel to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account. It is not a public, multi-user service. If you are that owner, “you” in this policy means you. If you reached this page for another reason, we do not seek to collect your TikTok data.

2. TikTok data we use

When you sign in with TikTok, the app requests only these scopes:

  • Login Kit / user.info.basic — basic profile information such as your TikTok username, display name, avatar, and a user identifier needed to recognize the connected account.
  • Content Posting API / video.upload — permission to upload a video file to the signed-in TikTok account as a draft. The app sends the video you choose and any caption or metadata you attach to that upload.

The app does not request access to your TikTok followers, direct messages, likes, browsing history, or any other scopes.

3. How that data is used and stored

Basic profile information is used only to confirm which TikTok account is connected and to show that account in the tool. Video files and upload permission are used only to send your chosen clip to that account as a draft for you to review and publish in TikTok. Tokens and profile details are stored only as long as they are needed to perform those uploads. They are not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell TikTok profile information, videos, tokens, or any other data from this app. We do not share that data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Processors and third parties

Sign-in and API calls go through Composio, an integration platform that handles the TikTok OAuth connection, and through TikTok / ByteDance, which provides Login Kit and the Content Posting API. Those providers process the data as needed to complete sign-in and uploads, under their own privacy policies. We do not otherwise share this data with third parties except if required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Profile and token data are kept only to perform uploads. When they are no longer needed for that purpose, they are deleted. You can also ask us to delete stored data at any time by emailing nevergiveupeverinlife@proton.me. After a verified request, we will delete the TikTok profile information and access tokens we hold, except for any limited record we must keep to satisfy a legal obligation.

7. Revoking access

You can disconnect the app at any time in your TikTok account settings (manage connected apps / authorized apps, or the equivalent TikTok privacy and security controls). Revoking access stops the app from reading your basic profile or uploading videos. You may also contact us at the address above and ask us to drop the stored connection.

8. Security

Access tokens are handled by Composio’s OAuth flow rather than being typed into chat or stored in public pages. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and you should revoke access if you believe a connection has been compromised.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age required by TikTok in your region.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated text on this page and change the date above. Where a change is material, we will also try to note it on the home page.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: nevergiveupeverinlife@proton.me
